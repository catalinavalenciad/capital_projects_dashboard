# Data Sources

## Synthetic Dataset
All project data was generated programmatically using Python's `faker` library. 
The dataset was designed to reflect realistic data center capital program structures 
based on six years of AEC industry experience.

## Data Center Market Locations
Project locations were selected based on real data center market concentration:
- Northern Virginia/Richmond: largest data center market in the US
- Dallas, TX: major Texas hub, favorable power and land costs
- Phoenix, AZ: fast-growing market, favorable climate for cooling
- Atlanta, GA: major Southeast hub
- Chicago, IL: central US connectivity
- Seattle, WA: proximity to major cloud providers
- Denver, CO: growing mountain west market
- Las Vegas, NV: expanding market, cheap land
- Charlotte, NC: emerging Southeast market
- Austin, TX: growing Texas market

## PMIS Schema Reference
Schema design informed by direct experience with:
- Procore (RFIs, submittals, change orders, contracts)
- Autodesk BIM360/Construction Cloud (project data management)
